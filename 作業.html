Aerodynamics (氣動力學)

v = √(vx² + vy²)
L = ½ρv²·S·CL ⟂ v
D = ½ρv²·S·CD ∥ −v
Lift is perpendicular to velocity; drag opposes it.
升力垂直於速度，阻力與速度反向。

Numerical Integration (數值積分)

aₓ = (−D·uₓ − L·u_y)/m
a_y = (−D·u_y + L·uₓ)/m − g
v ← v + a·Δt,  x ← x + v·Δt
Euler method, Δt = 1/240 s (歐拉法，時間步長 1/240 秒)。

Coefficients (係數模型)

CL = 0.2 + 3.5α + 0.02δ (stall 失速 > 15°)
CD = 0.04 + 0.15CL² + 0.003|δ|
g = 9.8, ρ = 1.225, S = 0.03 m², m = 10 g
α = Angle of Attack (攻角), δ = Elevator (升降舵)
